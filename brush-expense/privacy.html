Brush Expense — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026

Brush Expense ("the app", "we", "us"), published by Happie Max, is a personal and family expense tracker. It is local-first: your financial data lives on your own device, and any cloud backup uses your own Google Drive. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles and why.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo. This is used only to sign you in and to identify you to family members you choose to share with.
  • Financial data you create. Transactions, accounts, balances, budgets, categories and notes that you add. Stored locally on your device.
  • SMS messages. If you enable SMS expense tracking, the app reads your device's SMS inbox to detect bank/transaction alerts and pre-fill expense entries. SMS content is processed only on your device and is never uploaded to us or any third party. Only the transactions you explicitly approve are saved, and only the parsed details (amount, merchant, date) are kept — not the raw message.

2. How your data is stored

  • On your device. All your data is stored in a local database on your phone.
  • Optional Google Drive backup & family sync. If you turn these on, your data is written to your own Google Drive. The app uses the restricted drive.file scope, meaning it can only access files it created — never the rest of your Drive. We operate no server and never receive a copy of this data.

3. How your data is used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features — recording and showing your expenses, budgets and insights, and (if enabled) backing up or syncing with your family. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal or financial data with any third party. If you use family sync, your data is shared only with the family members you invite, through your own Google Drive folder.

5. Permissions we request

  • SMS (read): to detect bank transaction messages and pre-fill expenses, on-device only. Optional — the app works without it.
  • Notifications / foreground service: to show progress while scanning SMS in the background.
  • Internet: to sign in and to back up/sync with your Google Drive when you enable it.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device.
  • You can delete your Google Drive backups at any time from within the app or from Google Drive directly.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account permissions.

7. Security

Data stays on your device and in your own Google Drive. You can additionally protect the app with a device fingerprint/biometric lock in Settings.

8. Children

Brush Expense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
