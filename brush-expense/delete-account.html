Delete Your Account & Data

App: Brush Expense by Happie Max · Last updated: 31 May 2026

Brush Expense is local-first: your data is stored on your own device, and any cloud backup is kept in your own Google Drive. We operate no server and keep no copy of your data. You are always in full control and can delete everything at any time using the steps below.

Delete your data from the app

  1. Open Brush Expense → More → Reset Data. This permanently deletes all your data on the device (transactions, accounts, balances, budgets, categories and notes).
  2. Or simply uninstall the app — this removes all local app data from your device.

Delete your cloud backups

  1. In the app → More → Export & Backup → delete your backups from the list, or
  2. Go to Google Drive and delete the “ExpenseTrackerBackups” / Brush Expense sync folder.

Disconnect your Google account

Since sign-in uses Google, you can revoke the app's access anytime at your Google Account permissions page. We do not retain your name or email after you revoke access — we never stored them on a server.

What is deleted vs. kept

  • Deleted: all your financial data on the device and any Google Drive backups you remove.
  • Kept: nothing on our side — we hold no server-side copy of your data, so there is no additional retention period.
